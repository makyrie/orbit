03 — Voice in context

Sample passage

The joy of connecting with near strangers

Perihelion is a small tool for the friend group you already have, and the acquaintances you'd like to spend more time with.

It works like this: someone in your circle posts a thing they're doing — a Saturday hike, a casual dinner, an afternoon at the museum. People who've subscribed to them get a heads-up. Anyone interested says they're going. Anyone not interested doesn't have to say anything.

That's the whole product. There's no feed to scroll. There are no notifications begging you to come back. When you're done planning a hangout, you close the tab and go have it.
